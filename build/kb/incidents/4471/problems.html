The two problems

Month and day swapped in the shared parser. The parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first gets a flipped date, and its people see the booking on the wrong day. These are the app, email and booking-v2 form reports.

The booking form prints the wrong order for the tenant. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY. A day-first tenant reads that hint and types the month first, as it was told to, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. Same symptom, different cause, different fix: the form and the parser disagree about the same field. These are the form reports of the booking-v3 tenants.

What the desk should do

  • Split #4471 into two tickets, one per problem, each with the count in the table above.
  • Fix the parser to read the tenant’s date order instead of month first.
  • Fix booking-v3: it prints MM/DD/YYYY to tenants who write the day first.
  • Record a new decision. The merge rule in decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and a subject line cannot tell a swapped date from a shifted one. A decision is never edited after it is accepted, so this one is replaced, not corrected.
  • Look at the open questions above. They are counted in the parser group, where the desk files them, and no root cause is established for them yet.

Lessons

  • One ticket can hold more than one bug. Merge early, count once, but split by cause.
  • A duplicate is one id. The export gives the same report more than once.
  • A tag from intake is a guess. Read the tenant, and read the form the tenant runs.
